What's new in 1.0.1:
• Session groups: your Mac's groups show as tabs on your phone.
• Session rows match the Mac: agents, model and effort, pull requests and tasks at a glance, with swipe and long-press actions.
• Terminal: your Mac's Prosper colours, one Paste key for text and images, links that wrap across lines open whole, and a detached session comes back with Retry.
• Keyboards: an Extra Large key size, wider shift and delete, a low tap above space types a space, "?" and "!" on a hold of "." and ",", and the numpad's ABC, delete and return sit where the letters have them, with an option to show it without the slide.
• Themes: Royal Purple replaces Violet.
• Smarter autocorrect and touch correction, a sturdier clipboard history, and many fixes.
