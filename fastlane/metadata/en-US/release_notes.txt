What's new:
• Copy mode: drag over the terminal to select text natively, then edit what goes to the clipboard.
• Insert: compose text on your phone and send it to the session in one go.
• New compact terminal keyboard on iPhone, with key previews and common symbols one tap away.
• Shortcut bar is always visible, in two aligned rows, with a new default set of keys.
• Watched sessions show the same name in alerts as in your session list, and the list can show how many helper tasks are running.
• Typing is more reliable on weak connections: keystrokes are queued and delivered after a drop.
• Smoother terminal scrolling.
