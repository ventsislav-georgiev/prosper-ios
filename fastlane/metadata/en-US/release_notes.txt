What's new:
• Copy mode: drag over the terminal to select text natively, then edit what goes to the clipboard.
• Insert: compose text on your phone and send it to the session in one go.
• New session can start an agent session directly; set its command in Settings.
• New compact terminal keyboard on iPhone, with key previews and common symbols one tap away.
• Holding space in the Prosper keyboard now moves the caret up and down between lines too, not just sideways.
• Rearrange or hide the keys beside space in Settings, in the Prosper keyboard and the terminal keyboard alike; space widens to fill.
• Choose what a double space types in Settings: a period, an exclamation mark, a question mark, or nothing; the terminal keyboard does it too.
• Shortcut bar is always visible, in two aligned rows, with a new default set of keys.
• The session list can show how many helper tasks are running.
• The session list shows each session's open pull requests and task progress; press and hold a session's pull request line to open one.
• Session rows follow the Mac's Agent Island layout; choose which details they show in Settings.
• The session bell is replaced by live status: a Lock Screen and Dynamic Island card while agents work on your Mac, and a notification only when a session needs you, even with Prosper closed. Sign in on the Mac and the phone, then turn it on.
• Typing is more reliable on weak connections: keystrokes are queued and delivered after a drop.
• Smoother terminal scrolling.
• A drag along the right edge of the terminal scrolls even while the scroll wheel is hidden.
