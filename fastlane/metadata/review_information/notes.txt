App Review notes - Prosper Remote

WHAT THE APP DOES
Prosper attaches to terminal sessions on the user's own Mac (running the companion
Prosper desktop app) over the user's private Tailscale network, directly
device-to-Mac. It also ships the Prosper keyboard, a custom keyboard for any app.
Only Remote Wake and Live status need an account; all else works signed-out.

DEMO, NO MAC OR TAILSCALE NEEDED, NO SIGN-IN
  1. Launch the app. Tap "Remote Terminal".
  2. On "Machines", tap "Try the demo" (bottom of the list).
  3. Tap "Demo session": an interactive sample terminal; typed text is echoed
     (no shell runs on-device). The "?" button opens "How it works".

THE KEYBOARDS, WITHOUT A MAC
  1. Settings > General > Keyboard > Keyboards > Add New Keyboard > Prosper.
     Optionally tap Prosper > Allow Full Access.
  2. In any text field, tap the globe and pick Prosper. The toolbar toggle at
     the left of its top strip shows the edit keys; holding it opens a menu:
     Clipboard, Snippets, Quicklinks, Dictate, Rewrite, Commands, Emoji,
     Appearance, and a Hide Keyboard button.
  3. Settings in Prosper set themes, key size, numbers, languages, toolbar.
Full Access is used only to share the App Group with the Prosper app (settings,
toolbar, snippets, quicklinks, emoji recents, learned words), to keep the
clipboard history (iOS asks before a paste from another app unless Settings >
Apps > Prosper > Paste from Other Apps is Allow), and to open Prosper for
dictation. Commands: only when the user opens it or runs one, the keyboard
connects to the user's own Mac (the one Prosper last connected to, over their
Tailscale network) to list and run its commands; no other server. No typed
text leaves the device except a command's argument sent to that Mac; clipboard
history goes only to the user's own Mac, and only when sync is on on both.
Without Full Access it types with default settings.
Dictate (iOS 26) opens Prosper, which asks for the Microphone and Speech
Recognition; speech is transcribed on device by Apple's engine. Rewrite (Fix,
Rewrite, Summarize, iOS 26) uses Apple's on-device model and needs Apple
Intelligence on; without it the panel says so.

WHAT'S NEW IN 1.0
  - The Prosper keyboard (above), and the terminal's keyboard and two-row bar:
    open the Demo session and tap into the terminal.
  - Clipboard history synced with the Mac, Sync from Mac (snippets and
    quicklinks), session rows with agents, PRs and tasks, History, Chat and
    Commands (the session list's menu): all need a real Mac, so they are not
    in the demo, which lists sample sessions.
  - Live status and alerts with push notifications (below).
  - Share > Prosper, Shortcuts, Control Center controls, a Sessions widget.
No ads, tracking or purchases.

LIVE STATUS AND ALERTS (PUSH NOTIFICATIONS, LIVE ACTIVITY)
Opt-in, signed in only. The home screen then offers "Turn on notifications"
(Turn On / Not now) and Settings has a "Live status and alerts" switch; the
notification permission is asked only after Turn On. The app then sends its
APNs tokens and an install id to our server, which pushes a Live Activity
(Lock Screen, Dynamic Island) while the user's Mac has agents working, and a
notification only when a session needs the user; tapping opens it. Pushes are
triggered by the user's Mac, signed in to the same account, and carry session
names and states, never terminal content. Without a Mac nothing is pushed, so
it can't appear in review. Skip it: nothing else needs it.
Off or sign-out deletes the device's tokens from our server.

REMOTE WAKE
The Wake button appears only when a saved, real Mac can't be reached, so it is
not reachable in review. It needs sign-in and wakes only the account's Mac.

ACCOUNTS
Passwordless email magic link; no third-party login, so Sign in with Apple is
not required (4.8). Settings > Delete Account removes the account, wake data
and push tokens from our server.

ENCRYPTION
Standard TLS only (exempt).
