Privacy Policy — Prosper Remote

Last updated: 2026-11-03

Prosper Remote ("the app") lets you attach to terminal sessions running on your own Mac, optionally wake that Mac from sleep and get live status and alerts from it, and includes the Prosper keyboard for other apps. It is built to collect as little data as possible. You can use the terminal, the keyboard and the demo without an account. Only the optional Remote Wake and Live status features require signing in.

What we collect

If you never sign in

Nothing leaves your device for us. No account, no analytics, no advertising, no third-party tracking SDKs. Machine addresses you enter and terminal traffic stay between your device and your Mac (see below).

If you sign in to use Remote Wake or Live status

To wake a sleeping Mac, or to push you its status, the app talks to our account server. Signing in is passwordless: you enter your email address and we send a one-time magic link.

We store on the server only what the feature needs:

  • Email address — to identify your account. Stored in plain form, linked to your account.
  • A hashed session token — proves your device is signed in. We store only a SHA-256 hash, never the token itself.
  • Remote-wake configuration — for each of your Macs that has the feature enabled: a non-secret device identifier and the wake-poll cadence (how often the Mac checks for a wake request). Stored so another of your devices can tell whether a Mac can be woken and roughly how long it will take.
  • A transient wake flag — when you tap "Wake", we set a short-lived flag your Mac reads on its next scheduled check, then it wakes. It carries no personal data.

If you turn on Live status and alerts

This is off until you tap Turn On (or the switch in Settings) and allow notifications. Then the app sends our server:

  • Push tokens — the Apple Push Notification service tokens for this device's notifications and Live Activities, with a random install identifier for this device and, for a Live Activity, which of your Macs it shows. Stored linked to your account, so we can push to your devices only.

Your Mac, signed in to the same account, sends our server the names and states of its sessions (for example "working" or "needs you") and counts, never terminal content. We keep its latest status to decide what to push, and send it through Apple's push service to your devices as a Live Activity and, only when a session needs you, a notification. Turning the switch off, turning notifications off for the app, or signing out deletes this device's push tokens from the server.

We do not collect your machine addresses on the server — your Mac's Tailscale names and IPs are stored only on your device (see below). We do not track you across apps or sites, and we run no advertising.

Data stored on your device

Machine names and addresses you enter (your Mac's Tailscale name or IP) are stored locally on your device only, so you don't have to retype them. They are never transmitted to us. Your sign-in session is held in the device Keychain and never synced to iCloud.

Terminal traffic and your Mac

When you connect to your Mac, terminal input and output flow directly between your device and your Mac over your private Tailscale network. This traffic does not pass through any service operated by us — we cannot see it. The same goes for everything else the app exchanges with your Mac: clipboard history sync (if you turn on Sync with Mac on both), your Mac's snippets and quicklinks (Sync from Mac), shared items, chats with your Mac's local model, and your Mac's commands.

The Prosper keyboard

The keyboard makes no network requests and sends nothing to us. With Full Access it shares its settings, toolbar, emoji recents, learned words, snippets, quicklinks and clipboard history with the Prosper app through an App Group on this device; its clipboard history leaves the device only to your own Mac, as above.

Dictation and Rewrite

Dictation turns your speech into text on the device, with Apple's speech engine; the audio and text are not sent to us. Fix, Rewrite and Summarize, and Clean Up Dictation, use Apple's on-device model (Apple Intelligence); the text is not sent to us.

How long we keep data

  • Session tokens (hashed): up to about 365 days, or until you sign out (sign-out revokes them immediately on the server).
  • Push tokens: until you turn Live status off, sign out, or your sign-in session expires.
  • Your Mac's latest status: while your Mac publishes it; removed with your account.
  • Remote-wake configuration: up to about 1 year after a Mac last updated it, then automatically removed.
  • Transient wake flag: automatically expires within about 7 days (it is a one-time signal, normally consumed within minutes).
  • Account record after deletion: when you delete your account we remove your sessions, devices, settings, wake data, push tokens and Mac status. Your email is retained in a deletion record (tombstone) so the account cannot be silently re-created or re-used; it is not used for any other purpose.

If you have ever supported the project (a separate, optional one-time contribution), a supporter record may be retained for accounting and to display your supporter status.

Deleting your account

Open Settings → Delete Account in the app. This permanently deletes your account and remote-wake and live status data from the server (subject to the email tombstone described above) and signs you out on this device. It cannot be undone.

Tailscale

Connections rely on Tailscale, a third-party network. Your use of Tailscale is governed by Tailscale's own privacy policy: https://tailscale.com/privacy-policy

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone beyond what is described above.
